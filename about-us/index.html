About us

About Youth Face

A modern Indian skincare brand focused on simple, practical skincare for everyday routines.

Youth Face started with one idea: good skincare should be simple enough to keep doing. Instead of a shelf of products, we focus on a few well-chosen ingredients in products that fit into a busy day.

What we make

Our first product is Youth Face Beauty Cream, built around Kojic Acid and Alpha Arbutin for dark-spot care and a more even-looking tone. It is available as a single 25 g jar and in packs of two and three. We also make Youth Face Body Lotion, a light daily moisturiser for the body. More products are on the way.

Our promise

  • Genuine products, shipped directly by us. Buy from this site to avoid third-party copies.
  • Clear information on ingredients, usage and pricing, with no hidden costs.
  • Secure payment online, or Cash on Delivery with a small advance.
  • Real support on WhatsApp, before and after you buy.

Who we are

Youth Face is run by Beauty Mart from Bhatkal, Karnataka. Every order is packed by our team and handed to a trusted courier.
