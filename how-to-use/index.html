How to use Youth Face

Simple steps for the Beauty Cream and the Body Lotion.

Youth Face Beauty Cream

  1. Wash your face with a gentle cleanser and pat it dry.
  2. Take a small amount, about the size of a pea, for the face and neck.
  3. Massage gently until absorbed. Avoid the eye area.
  4. Use once or twice a day. In the morning, always finish with sunscreen.

Tips

  • Patch test on a small area for 24 hours before first use.
  • Use it regularly for several weeks before judging.
  • Stop use if redness, burning or irritation continues, and consult a dermatologist.

Youth Face Body Lotion

  1. Apply a generous amount to clean, dry body skin.
  2. Massage gently until absorbed.
  3. Use daily, or whenever your skin feels dry.
