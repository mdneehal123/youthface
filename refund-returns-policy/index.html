Refund & Returns

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Return window

Return requests are accepted within 7 days of delivery.

What can be returned

  • Wrong, damaged, defective or materially different items.
  • Unused, unopened items in original condition with original packaging and seals.

Hygiene products

Opened, used, tested or tampered products cannot be returned, unless they arrived damaged, defective or incorrect. Your legal rights for such products are not affected.

How to request a return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 99808 81230 within 7 days of delivery.
  2. Share your order details and photos of the product, packaging and shipping label (a short video if needed).
  3. Wait for approval and instructions before sending anything back.

Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method after the returned item is received and checked. Banks usually take 5 to 7 business days to show the amount. Shipping costs are refunded when the error was ours.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before they are packed or dispatched. Once dispatched, the return process applies.

Refused or undelivered orders

For Cash on Delivery orders refused at delivery or undeliverable because of incorrect details, the ₹99 advance is not refunded.

Purchases elsewhere

This policy applies to orders placed on this website. Purchases from other sellers follow that seller's policy.
