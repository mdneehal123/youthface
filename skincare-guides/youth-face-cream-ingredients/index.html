Youth Face Cream ingredients: what is in it?

The two key ingredients, what each is used for, and how to read the full list on your pack.

Youth Face Beauty Cream is built around two key ingredients: Kojic Acid and Alpha Arbutin. Both are widely used in creams made for dark-spot care and a more even-looking tone. The full ingredient list is printed on every pack, so you can check it before you use it.

The two key ingredients

IngredientWhy it is usedGood to know
Kojic AcidUsed in creams for dark-spot care and uneven-looking toneAn active ingredient: patch test first and use sunscreen
Alpha ArbutinUsed for a brighter-looking, more even complexionKnown for being gentle; often paired with Kojic Acid

How to read the full list on your pack

  1. Find the ingredient list on the jar or box. Ingredients are listed from the most to the least used.
  2. Look for anything you know you react to, such as fragrance or a particular preservative.
  3. If you are unsure about an ingredient, ask a dermatologist before using the cream.

Before you start

Do a patch test behind the ear for 24 hours, use a pea-sized amount, and wear sunscreen every morning. Results take weeks, not days: see how long a dark spot cream takes to work.
