How to order Youth Face Cream: COD, online payment and tracking

Step by step: choosing a pack, paying online or on delivery, delivery times and tracking your parcel.

Ordering from youthfacebeautycream.com takes about a minute. You can pay online or choose Cash on Delivery, and shipping is free on every order.

Step by step

  1. Choose your pack on the shop page: Pack of 1, 2 or 3, or the Cream + Body Lotion combo. See the price list.
  2. Add to cart and go to checkout.
  3. Enter your name, mobile number, address and pincode. The pincode shows your city and an estimated delivery date.
  4. Choose how to pay: online by UPI, card or netbanking, or Cash on Delivery.
  5. Confirm. You see your order details on screen. Cash on Delivery orders also get a Delhivery tracking number straight away.

Cash on Delivery

  • Nothing to pay in advance.
  • You pay the full amount in cash to the courier when the parcel arrives.
  • No extra Cash on Delivery fee.
  • Available on most pincodes. If your pincode is not eligible, the checkout tells you, and you can pay online instead.

Please keep your phone on and the exact amount ready, so the delivery goes smoothly. Please order Cash on Delivery only when you are sure you want the product, so the parcel is accepted at the door.

Pay online

Pay by UPI, card or netbanking through Razorpay, on a secure payment page. You get a payment ID, which you can also use on the Track Order page.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are packed and handed to the courier in 1 to 3 business days. After dispatch, delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days, depending on your pincode. These are estimates, and weather, festivals or courier delays can add time.

Track your order

Open Track Order and use your mobile number with your pincode, or your order number, payment ID or tracking number.

Returns

If an item arrives damaged, leaking or wrong, we replace it or refund you in full. Sealed, unused items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Opened or used cosmetics cannot be returned. Read the full returns policy.

Prefer WhatsApp?

You can also message us on WhatsApp at +91 99808 81230 and we will help you place the order. Every order is packed in sealed packaging by our team in Bhatkal, Karnataka.
