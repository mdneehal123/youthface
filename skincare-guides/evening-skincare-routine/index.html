Evening skincare routine

A calm, simple routine before bed.

The evening routine is about cleaning off the day and giving your treatment cream time to work while you sleep.

1. Clean off the day

Sunscreen, sweat, dust and makeup need to come off. Use a gentle cleanser; if you wear heavy makeup or water-resistant sunscreen, cleanse twice. Pat dry.

2. Apply your cream

Apply Youth Face Beauty Cream to clean, dry skin. Night is a good time for your treatment step because there is no sun exposure afterwards.

3. Let it rest

Give the cream a few minutes to absorb before lying down, and change pillowcases often, since they collect oil and dirt.

Common mistakes

  • Too many actives at once. Layering several strong products at night is a common cause of irritation. Keep it to one treatment cream.
  • Skipping the cleanse. Cream applied over the day's sunscreen and dust works less well.
  • Scrubbing hard. Rough exfoliation makes skin sensitive. Gentle is enough.

Pair this with the daytime routine and you have the whole day covered.
