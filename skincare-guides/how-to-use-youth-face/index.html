How to use Youth Face products

Simple directions for the Beauty Cream and Body Lotion.

Both Youth Face products are made for a simple daily routine. Here is exactly how to use each one.

Youth Face Beauty Cream (face)

  1. Wash your face with a gentle cleanser and pat dry.
  2. Take a pea-sized amount for the face, a little more if you include the neck.
  3. Dot it on the forehead, cheeks and chin, then massage in gently until absorbed. Avoid the eye area.
  4. Use once a day to start, then twice a day (morning and night) if your skin is comfortable.
  5. Every morning, finish with a broad-spectrum sunscreen.

One 25 g jar usually lasts around a month of daily use, depending on how much you apply, which is why many customers choose the Pack of 2 or Pack of 3 to keep the routine going without a gap.

Youth Face Body Lotion (body)

  1. Apply a generous amount to clean, dry body skin, ideally after a bath.
  2. Massage in until absorbed.
  3. Use daily, or whenever skin feels dry.

The Body Lotion is a separate product for the body. Use the Beauty Cream on your face.

Before first use

  • Patch test on a small area for 24 hours.
  • Do not apply on broken, sunburnt or freshly waxed skin.
  • Stop if irritation continues, and consult a dermatologist.
